    What is simulated, and what is approximate
    • Every round is drawn from the simulator's own distributions (charge, bullet mass and diameter, case, primer sensitivity, rare physical defects). Nothing decides "jam now": a stoppage happens only when a force, energy, travel or time inequality in the mechanism fails for that round in that gun.
    • Interior ballistics, the AK gas/carrier cycle and the Makarov blowback cycle are a line-for-line port of the Python simulators, with the same constants and calibration (AK nominal 715 m/s, Makarov 315 m/s / 160 MPa).
    • "Fresh production" ammunition is an assumption: AK defect rates at one tenth of the aged-surplus table; Makarov drier storage, cleaner primers and less handling damage. "Dirty" and "sandy" set the sim's fouling and grit indices directly.
    • External ballistics are approximate: point-mass G7 drag (7.62x39 M43 BC about 0.138, 9x18 about 0.06), standard air, no wind, plus a fixed rifle/pistol precision cone (1.0 / 2.0 MOA per axis). Zero: AK 100 m, Makarov 25 m.
    • The 3D motion follows the sim's carrier/slide travel and timing; hammer arcs, bolt rotation and part poses during a stoppage are approximate. The Makarov lock time is derived from its hammer inertia and spring torque (not computed by the Python sim).

Targets: steel poppers at 200 m, tank hulks at 500 m and 1 km, and a 5 km area target for the Grad.
    • Changelog, 20 Nov: Tempest-6 and Tempest-7. The rotary guns are shown under new names, tagged PREMIUM (fictional, outside the historical timeline): the former Vykhor-6 is the "Tempest-6 Gatling" (the older model, link #asset=gatling as before), and the Vykhor-7 from the board is a second entry, the "Tempest-7 Gatling" (#asset=tempest7). The Tempest-7 uses the board's vykhor7-gun model (824 KB, untextured materials) and fires with the same behaviour as the Tempest-6: spin-up, fire from the barrel at the top, spin-down, each bolt riding its cam. Approximation: it shares the Tempest-6's rotary-gun sim, so its rates, belt, ballistics and spent cases are the Tempest-6's 5.56 mm figures, not a 7.62 mm model, and its cut-away view shows the assembled model.
    • Changelog, 20 Nov (phase 2): support weapons fire (the "Mortar 82 / HMG 12.7 / RPG-7 / Javelin" row). 82 mm 2B14 mortar: high-angle fire, charges 1 to 4, auto-lay to 45-85 deg, a time-of-flight readout and a SPLASH countdown. 12.7 mm NSV on a 6T7 tripod. RPG-7 with the PG-7VL (115 m/s launch, sustainer to about 295 m/s, self-destruct at 4.5 s). Javelin: 1.5 s seeker lock, soft launch, climb to a top-attack apex and a dive onto the target's roof. The mortar and the NSV are clean stand-in models (no model of them exists yet); the RPG-7 with its PG-7VL and the Javelin launcher and missile are the repo's hero models. Approximate: drag figures, sustainer and guidance laws are estimates.
    • Changelog, 20 Nov (phase 3): build mode and NPC minds (build.js, in the weapons panel of any vehicle or support weapon). Foxhole 60 s, sandbag wall 90 s, small bunker 300 s at one worker (x10 option), built in stages from a supply crate (sandbags, timber, nets; a plot waits when materials are short); tap the ground to place. Structures stop the range's rounds, take damage and can be destroyed. With NPCs on, a private digs in by himself when shells land near him, loses morale under fire, and may surrender with a white flag (2.5x as likely for the Russian side); Take captive +100, a round on a surrendering soldier -200. Approximate: box collision, simple shapes, a one-number morale model; NPCs do not shoot back.
    • Vehicle weapons are APPROXIMATE: point-mass flight with quadratic drag fitted to published muzzle velocities and typical velocity loss per km, gravity, standard air, no wind; a random precision cone per weapon; rocket thrust 21.4 kN for 1.8 s with burn-off mass (estimated from the 690 m/s top speed); ATGM guidance is a simple steer-to-line-of-sight; reload and carousel replenish times are shortened (a real Grad reload takes about 7 minutes); the auto-lay is an ideal fire-control solution. Projectiles use the repo's ammo models (125 mm, 100 mm, 9M22 hero); MG and 30 mm rounds are drawn as tracers only. The tank hulks and steel poppers are simple stand-ins.
